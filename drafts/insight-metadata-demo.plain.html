Corporate whistleblowing in Australia sits at an inflection point. This article—authored directly in the page—pairs the Content Fragment metadata above with a body that editors can shape without touching the source fragment.

Why author the body in the page?

Decoupling the narrative from the Content Fragment lets legal and marketing teams iterate on wording, add page-specific context, and publish quickly, while the fragment continues to own the canonical metadata: title, authors, publication date and taxonomy.

What still comes from the fragment

  • The hero kicker, headline and published date
  • The byline and “Meet The Authors” band
  • The intro lead paragraph and the Categories taxonomy

Practical implications

Because the body is ordinary authored content, it supports the full range of Edge Delivery authoring—headings, lists, links and images—and is decorated by the same global styles as the rest of the site.

For teams, this means a single Content Fragment can back many pages, each with a bespoke body, without duplicating the underlying metadata.
